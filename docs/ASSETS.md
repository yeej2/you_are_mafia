# Asset register

## Table illustration
Built-in image generation. Source prompt: Wide painterly graphic novel illustration of six suspicious fictional adults around a circular poker table in a 1930s Art Deco secret society room, crimson/charcoal/cream palette, overhead pendant light and cigarette haze, dark edges for UI, no text or real people.
Used as `public/noir-table.webp`, converted to WebP for web delivery.

## Music and effects
Original additive-synthesis composition, 32 seconds, 22.05kHz mono WAV. Sparse minor-key arpeggios, low drone and short echo. Generated reproducibly by `scripts/create-audio.py`. Vote, dawn and night cues are short synthesized tones. Music starts only following an explicit button click, loops and can be muted.

## Brand
Working title: Midnight Alibi. Charcoal, crimson and antique ivory palette; serif headlines with sans-serif controls. Simple geometric spade icon. No claim of trademark availability or clearance.

## Native icons and splash screens
The geometric spade brand icon is included in Android density variants and the iOS 1024px app icon, with dark branded splash screens.

## Simulation character atlas

`public/studio/vivian-expressions.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-03 and copied into the project. It contains four equal cells (neutral, speaking, skeptical, shocked) for the original fictional Vivian Voss. The animation samples one cell at a time. Buildings, bodies, role props, camera motions and action indicators are drawn by the code-native canvas renderer.

Final prompt: "Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one game character expression atlas for Midnight Alibi, a configurable animated Mafia simulation. Create a square 2x2 sprite sheet with four perfectly equal square cells, no gaps, no borders and absolutely no text. Same original fictional character Vivian Voss in all four cells, head and shoulders centered in each cell, identical scale, hair and wardrobe. Vivian is an elegant calculating noir socialite with a sharp black bob haircut, expressive eyes, burgundy jacket and gold earrings. Appealing low-poly toy-like 3D game character illustration, chunky simplified shapes, clean readable silhouette, softly lit. Top left neutral closed mouth, top right speaking mouth open, bottom left skeptical narrowed eyes, bottom right shocked worried expression. Solid very dark plum background in each cell. No weapons, no logos. Keep whole head and shoulders inside each cell. This atlas is sampled cell-by-cell by the animation renderer."

Future per-persona assets are generated by the application server using the prompt in `lib/media-provider.ts`. They are cached for reuse. The reference video is [10 AIs Play Mafia by Turing Games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhBtg-lyKdo); no footage, logos, character art, or audio from it was copied.

## Creature expression atlas
`public/studio/vivian-creature-expressions.png` was generated with the built-in image tool. Prompt: One square four-expression atlas, strict 2x2 grid, no borders, gutters, or lettering. Original cat-moth creature Vivian Voss, charcoal velvet fur, pointed fluffy ears, moth antennae, amber eyes, aubergine cheek tufts, gold pendant; elegant and calculating; rounded handcrafted stop-motion clay and felt; no block or voxel shapes. Identical scale and identity: neutral, speaking, skeptical, shocked. Deep plum backdrop. The renderer samples each quadrant. Uploaded photos instead use a user-adjustable circular crop and stay on the device.
